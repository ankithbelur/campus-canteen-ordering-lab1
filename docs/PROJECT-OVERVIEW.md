# Project Overview — Campus Canteen Ordering System

## 1. Introduction

The Campus Canteen Ordering System is a proposed software solution designed to simplify food ordering in a college canteen. It aims to reduce waiting time, make menu information easily accessible, and help canteen staff manage incoming orders efficiently.

## 2. Problem Statement

In a traditional college canteen, students may have to wait in long queues, ask staff about menu availability, calculate their order totals manually, and repeatedly check the status of their orders. Manual order processing can also make it difficult for staff to organize incoming orders during busy periods.

## 3. Objectives

- Provide students with an easy way to view the canteen menu.
- Display food item names, prices, and availability.
- Allow students to select multiple food items and specify quantities.
- Calculate the total cost of an order automatically.
- Allow students to submit orders and view their order status.
- Help canteen staff view incoming orders and update their status.

## 4. Scope

### In Scope

- Viewing available menu items.
- Selecting food items and quantities.
- Calculating the order total.
- Submitting an order and displaying confirmation.
- Tracking order status.
- Allowing canteen staff to review orders and update their status.

### Out of Scope

- Real payment gateway integration in the initial prototype.
- Off-campus food delivery.
- Advanced inventory forecasting.
- A finalized order cancellation feature until its policy is confirmed.

## 5. Stakeholders

- **Students:** Browse the menu, place orders, and check order status.
- **Canteen Staff:** View incoming orders and update their status.
- **College Administration:** Oversee canteen operations.
- **Development Team:** Gather requirements, design, implement, and test the system.

## 6. Expected Benefits

- Reduced waiting time for students.
- Easier access to menu information.
- Fewer manual calculation errors.
- Better organization of incoming orders.
- Improved visibility of order progress.

## 7. Assumptions and Constraints

- Students have access to a device and an internet connection.
- Menu prices and availability are maintained accurately.
- Order status updates are provided by canteen staff.
- The initial version is a learning prototype and does not process real payments.
- Performance targets must be validated before real-world deployment.

## 8. Conclusion

The Campus Canteen Ordering System aims to make campus food ordering more convenient for students and easier to manage for canteen staff. This project overview establishes the initial scope and objectives and will serve as a foundation for detailed requirements gathering and software planning.
